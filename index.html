What to expect

Real results depend on your hardware and what's already configured — here's an honest range by tier, not a single made-up average.

OLDER / BUDGET GPUS (e.g. GTX 1650–1660 class)

Usually the biggest jumps. These systems tend to be running the most background overhead relative to their headroom, so expect toward the higher end of the 15-40% range.

MID-RANGE GPUS (e.g. RTX 3060–4060 class)

Typically 15-25% more FPS, plus a noticeably steadier frametime graph — less stutter — from the network and scheduling tweaks specifically.

HIGH-END GPUS (e.g. RTX 4070+ / enthusiast)

Smaller raw FPS gains since there's less headroom to begin with — the real value here is fewer occasional frame drops, not a big top-line number.
